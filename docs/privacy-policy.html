Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026
Effective date: September 30, 2026

Card Trace (the “App”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App and the choices you have. In this policy, “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the developer of Card Trace.

1. Information Collection and Use

Card Trace is an offline puzzle game that does not require registration or an account. During normal use, the App does not collect or upload your personal information, gameplay records, or device identifiers to developer-operated servers.

To save your progress, resume games, provide replays, and remember your preferences, the App stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Game progress, level drafts, card placements, and related gameplay actions;
  • Game notes, clue markings, and hint usage;
  • Completion records, completion times, and most recent play times;
  • Preferences such as haptic feedback and text size.

The App processes this data on your device. It is not used for advertising, user profiling, or tracking across apps.

2. Data Storage and Retention

Gameplay data is stored in the App’s local storage on your device. The App may keep local backup copies of saved games to support recovery. It does not provide a developer-operated cloud synchronization service.

If you enable Apple’s device backup features, App data may be included in backups and restored from them, depending on your system settings and Apple’s service rules. We cannot access your device backups.

Local data is generally retained until you delete the App or the relevant data.

3. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

The current version does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. We do not sell, rent, or actively share your locally stored gameplay data with third parties.

When you use Apple services, such as the App Store or device backups, Apple’s handling of information is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

4. Device Permissions

The App does not require access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone. It does not request permission to track you across apps for advertising.

5. Information You Provide When Contacting Us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, message content, and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information only to address your inquiry, provide support, and conduct necessary follow-up communication.

Email is transmitted and stored through the relevant email service providers. We retain support correspondence only for as long as necessary to address these matters, or as required by applicable law. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is unrelated to your inquiry.

6. Your Choices and Control Over Data

You can change preferences within the App. To remove the App and its local data, use the “Delete App” option in iOS. The “Offload App” option may retain App data.

Data already included in device backups must be managed separately through the relevant Apple backup settings.

Because we cannot access your locally stored gameplay data, we cannot retrieve, export, or delete it on your behalf. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you have provided to us by email, contact us using the address below.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App’s gameplay features do not collect personal information for the developer from any user, including children. If you believe a child has provided unnecessary personal information to us by email, please contact us so we can review and appropriately address the matter.

8. Data Security

The App uses the application storage isolation provided by iOS to store local data and maintains local save backups to help reduce the impact of accidental data corruption. No storage or communication method can guarantee absolute security. Please protect your device and its backups.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App’s features change. We will revise the “Last updated” date above when we do so. If we introduce network connectivity, data collection, or third-party services, we will provide appropriate information before enabling the relevant features and obtain your consent where required.
